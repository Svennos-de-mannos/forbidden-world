About this world

Forgotten and Forbidden

History has a violent way of repeating itself, and the sins of the past rarely stay buried.

Two hundred years ago, the grand Kingdom of King Hemmeth I collapsed in a single night when his towering castle was swallowed by the earth, leaving behind a fragmented realm and a blood debt unpaid. For 2 centuries, the world moved on, building modern industries, bustling towns, and new lives over the forgotten catacombs. But beneath the surface, the gears of ancient malice never stopped turning.

Today, the echoes of that long-forgotten empire are shattering the present day across two different fronts, drawing unsuspecting souls into a convergence that could rip the world apart at its seams.
